Privacy Policy

Effective date: [FILL IN ON PUBLISH]

StackLens ("the app") is a personal collection-tracking app for physical silver items. This policy describes, plainly and specifically, what data the app handles and what it does not.

The short version: StackLens does not have user accounts, does not run analytics or crash reporting, and does not sell or share your data with advertisers. Almost everything you enter stays on your device. The one exception is described in full below.

Data stored only on your device

Your collection data never leaves your device except as described in the next section. This includes:

  • Item details you enter: description, purity, weight, quantity, status, dates, and any notes (including a certification number field, if you choose to fill one in — StackLens does not verify certification numbers against any registry; see "Identification is not authentication" in the Terms of Use)
  • Purchase and cost information you enter for each item (price, tax, shipping, fees, and any source name you type in)
  • Photos you take or choose of your items and receipts — stored as image files in the app's private storage area on your device
  • Goals you set and your notification preferences
  • A history log of item splits and merges

None of this is synced to iCloud, backed up to any server we operate, or visible to us in any form. We have no database of user data — nothing to lose, breach, or hand over, because it never reaches us.

Data sent off your device: the optional Scan feature

StackLens's "Scan" feature can suggest what an item might be from a photo you take of it. If you use this feature, the photo you capture is sent over an encrypted connection to a server we operate, which forwards it to a third-party AI service (Google's Gemini API) to generate suggestions, which are returned to your device.

Specifically:

  • Only the image itself is sent — no name, email, device identifier, account ID, or any other identifying information, because the app has no accounts and collects none of that to begin with.
  • Our server does not log, store, retain, or use this image for any purpose other than generating your one identification response. There is no database on our server; each request exists only for the moment it takes to process it.
  • Google processes the image under its own API terms to generate the suggestion. We do not control Google's infrastructure and encourage you to review Google's own privacy terms for its Gemini API if you want those details.
  • This feature is entirely optional. You can add every item to your collection by typing its details in yourself, without ever using Scan and without any photo ever leaving your device.
  • Suggestions from this feature are guesses, not verified identifications or authentications — see the in-app disclaimers on that screen.

Subscriptions and payment

If you subscribe to StackLens Pro, your purchase is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or collect your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID — Apple processes the transaction and simply tells the app whether you currently hold an active subscription. Apple's own privacy policy governs the data it collects for the purchase itself.

Notifications

Any reminders or alerts you enable (a weekly reminder, or a notification when you complete a goal) are scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's local notification system. No push notification service is used, and no notification data is sent to or generated by any server.

Backups

You can create an encrypted backup file of your collection, protected by a passphrase you choose. This file is created entirely on your device and is never uploaded anywhere by the app — where it goes next (saved to Files, emailed, AirDropped, etc.) is entirely your choice, using your device's standard share sheet. We never see this file or your passphrase. If you lose your passphrase, the backup cannot be recovered — not by you, and not by us, because we never had it.

Children's privacy

StackLens is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children, in part because it does not collect identifying data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and, for any material change to how data is handled, we will provide notice in the app.
