Frequently asked questions

Does StackLens require an account or internet connection?

No account is needed for anything. Adding items, tracking values, setting goals, exporting, and backing up all work fully offline. The only feature that needs a connection is the optional Scan photo-identification suggestion — everything else works without one.

What does the Scan feature do with my photo?

It sends the photo you capture to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI to suggest what the item might be. Nothing is logged or stored on our end — see the Privacy Policy for full detail. You can always skip Scan and enter items manually instead.

Are Scan's suggestions verified identifications?

No. They're AI-generated guesses meant to speed up filling in the item form — always review and correct them yourself. StackLens never claims to authenticate or grade an item.

Where do market prices come from?

Reference prices shown are for informational estimates only, not a live market feed, and should not be relied on for trading decisions.

How do I back up my collection?

Settings → Create Backup generates an encrypted file protected by a passphrase you choose. Save it wherever you like (Files, email, AirDrop, etc.) using the share sheet. To restore it, use Settings → Restore from Backup on the same or a new device. If you lose your passphrase, the backup cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

What happens to my data if I cancel StackLens Pro?

Nothing in your existing collection is hidden, locked, or deleted. The free tier only limits adding a 51st+ non-archived item — archiving an item you no longer need to track always frees up room.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. Individual items can be deleted from within the app at any time.

Is StackLens financial advice?

No. See the Terms of Use — StackLens is a record-keeping and estimation tool, not investment, tax, or legal advice.
